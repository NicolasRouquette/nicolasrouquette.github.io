Nicolas F. Rouquette

Principal Computer Scientist, Jet Propulsion Laboratory, California Institute of Technology

Formal methods and model-based systems engineering for space systems: Lean 4 formalizations, SysML v2, ontologies, assume-guarantee contracts, F´ flight software.

I work on the foundations that make engineering models trustworthy: what a model means, what a tool is allowed to conclude from it, and how to prove that the conclusion is right.

At JPL I co-lead openCAESAR, an ontology-based approach to model-based systems engineering built on the Ontological Modeling Language (OML), and I contributed to Pacti, a library for assume-guarantee contracts. Earlier I worked on the Deep Space 1 Remote Agent experiment, model-based diagnosis, and the SysML–Modelica transformation specification.

Lately most of my work is in Lean 4: machine-checked parsers and semantics, formalizations of metrology (kinds of property, units, uncertainty), SysML v2 execution semantics, and provable observability for F´ flight software.

Interests

  • Formal methods (Lean 4, Dafny, Viper, Stainless)
  • Model-based systems engineering (SysML v2, OML, openCAESAR)
  • Ontologies and semantic web for engineering
  • Assume-guarantee contracts and compositional design
  • Metrology, units, and uncertainty quantification
  • Flight software (F´) and observability
